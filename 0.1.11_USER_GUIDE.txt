Draw Catchments — quick guide
Created by Omar Brahimi

Load your groundwater contour line layer and a well/monitoring-point layer.
Choose the contour layer, its hydraulic-head field, and one point or a well group.

YOUR ARC ENDPOINTS
Click Place arc endpoints on map, then click the two positions around the well.
The orange preview shows the closure. The same points start the two uphill sides.
The default depth is half their separation; Arc depth changes the bulge.
This is your geometric closing boundary, not a pumping-derived capture limit.
The final polygon must contain the selected well(s).

AUTOMATIC
Draw catchment follows the head gradient. Automatic extent expansion now continues
beyond the initial radius when the upstream paths meet its edge and more source
contours exist. Highest connected contour closes on a real shared contour and
does not certify a divide. To specify where to reach, use Marked upstream target
area or Custom boundary layer. The target/boundary must be reached by both sides.

MANUAL
Draw straight boundary steps manually. Closest feasible to 90 degrees is the default:
prefer exact normals, otherwise show the actual angle of a clear straight candidate.
Only the well closure is curved. Add next contour accepts the orange preview;
clicking farther includes intervening contours. Exact 90 degrees only is optional.
Switch side, finish both sides on the same contour, then Finish & export.
Place arc endpoints in the floating panel before drawing, or undo both sides first.

SAVE
Finish & export saves a polygon. Save drawing draft saves unfinished drawing.
GeoPackage preserves the polygon, original, paths, endpoints and closing boundaries.

INSTALL
Plugins > Manage and Install Plugins > Install from ZIP.
Select the supplied ZIP, replace the existing installation, and restart QGIS.
Open Vector > Draw Catchments or its toolbar icon.
